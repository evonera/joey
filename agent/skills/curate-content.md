Use when the user wants content inspired by current events, industry news, trending topics, or when they ask you to "curate", "find news", "research trends", or "draft something timely."

1. Use `connection_search` with queries like "news" or "search" or "trends" to discover available Composio tools for finding current information.

2. Research topics relevant to the brand's voice and posting goals. Look for:
   - Industry news and developments
   - Trending conversations on social media
   - Content that the brand can offer a unique perspective on

3. From the results, identify 2-3 stories worth the brand commenting on.

4. For each story, draft a post using `draft_post` with platform-appropriate content that adds the brand's perspective rather than just repeating the news.

5. Save a strategy insight with `remember` noting what trend you identified, why it is relevant to the brand, and what content approach worked best.
