Use this skill when the user asks to create, storyboard, or draft a multi-slide carousel for Instagram (1080x1350 4:5 or 1080x1080 1:1) or LinkedIn document carousels.

## Architecture of a High-Retention Carousel

A winning carousel typically contains 5 to 8 slides with clear visual momentum:

1. **Slide 1 (Hero Hook)**:
   - Massive contrast headline (12 words or fewer).
   - A bold assertion or high-stakes question.
   - Clear swipe indicator (e.g. "Swipe →" or 1/7).
2. **Slide 2 (The Shift / The Context)**:
   - Sets the stakes. Why what people normally do doesn't work.
3. **Slides 3-6 (The Core Meat / Actionable Insights)**:
   - ONE idea per slide.
   - Minimal text: 20-35 words max per slide.
   - Use bold highlights, callout boxes, or comparison tables (Before vs After).
4. **Slide 7 (The Recap / Synthesis)**:
   - A single takeaway summary diagram or checklist.
5. **Slide 8 (Call to Action / Save & Share)**:
   - "Save this for your next launch" or "Drop a comment with your thoughts".

## Output Format

For each slide provide:

- **Slide Number & Title**
- **Headline Text** (Large display)
- **Subtext / Body** (Short, punchy)
- **Visual / Layout Recommendation** (e.g., "Dark background #0a0908, yellow accent pill, screenshot mock").
