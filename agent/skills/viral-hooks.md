Use this skill when the user asks for video hooks, opening lines, scroll-stoppers, or ways to capture viewer attention in the first 2-3 seconds for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or Twitter video.

## Objective

Generate 5 distinct, high-impact hook variations based on proven short-form cognitive triggers.

## The 5 Hook Archetypes

1. **Pattern Interrupt**: Discards standard pleasantries or expected visuals. Immediately says or displays something unexpected, contrarian, or counter-intuitive.
   - _Example_: "Stop designing carousels in Figma until you see this 10-second shortcut."
2. **Negative Constraint / Fear of Missing Out (FOMO)**: Frames what the viewer is doing wrong or what they will lose by scrolling past.
   - _Example_: "The one reason your posts get 200 views and plateau."
3. **Curiosity Gap / Open Loop**: Reveals a tantalizing result without revealing the mechanism until slide/second 2.
   - _Example_: "I tested 100 AI hooks last month. Only 1 format actually drove followers."
4. **Proof / Credibility First**: Leads with hard data, a screenshot, or a concrete metric.
   - _Example_: "This 30-second reel did 1.2M views with zero ad spend. Here's the exact script."
5. **Story / Direct Address**: Speaks to a specific persona's lived pain point.
   - _Example_: "If you're a solo founder trying to post 3x a week, watch this before you burn out."

## Output Format

Always present the 5 hooks clearly labeled by archetype, followed by a 1-sentence **Visual Cue** (what should appear on screen during those first 2 seconds) and the **Bridge** (the transition sentence to the core content).
