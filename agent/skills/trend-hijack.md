Use this skill when a breaking event, viral meme, or trending conversation emerges, and the user wants to join the discourse before the 24-hour relevance window expires.

## Speed vs Taste Balance

- **Speed**: The value of trendjacking drops by 50% every 6 hours after the spike.
- **Taste**: Never hijack tragedies, sensitive controversies, or topics that would compromise the brand's credibility.

## 3 Reaction Formats

1. **The Perspective Flip**: Take the popular consensus and provide the counter-argument or behind-the-scenes reality from your industry.
2. **The Analogy / Metaphor**: Relate the trending event to a common problem your product or service solves.
3. **The Meme Template**: Map the viral visual/punchline format directly onto a relatable situation in your industry.

## Rules of Engagement

- Keep the lead post under 200 characters for punchiness.
- Include a visual concept suggestion (e.g. meme graphic or short video clip).
- Conclude with a conversation starter question that encourages comments.
